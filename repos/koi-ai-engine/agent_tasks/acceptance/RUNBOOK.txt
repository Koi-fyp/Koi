* Each request also wakes two T4 GPU containers (chat + crisis) that then idle-bill for 5 min (chat) and 15 min
  (crisis) after the last request (see the next section).

READING THE RESULTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* cold_probe.txt: "hop N ... status=303 Location=<host><path>" lines = Modal's 150 s poll redirects. The time of
  "first SSE event" on a cold system is the user-visible wake-up time; compare with 60-190 s.
* Verdicts: PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE (the precondition did not occur, e.g. the text did not score HIGH) / ERROR.
  The crisis model is an emotion proxy: the category of any synthetic text is not guaranteed; it is recorded as seen.
* Watch especially: H10 "HIGH risk event without a handoff" (score missed the 1 s director wait),
  C12/C15 "crisis_resolved ignored" (resolving text scored HIGH), X4 silent reset, risk_exactly_once,
  state_immediately_before_done, full_text_equals_deltas, trace_present (must be False on the final deploy).
